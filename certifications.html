Quality & Compliance

Our Certifications & Accreditations

Quality, safety and innovation are engineered into every MarkEn system — from design and manufacturing to after-sales service — and independently verified against the international standards healthcare institutions rely on.

Certification Overview

Built to Global Standards

MarkEn's quality management system is certified to ISO 9001:2015 and to ISO 13485:2016 — the dedicated standard for medical device manufacturers — from NABCB under IAF. Our manufacturing also holds ISO 14001 environmental and ISO 45001 occupational health & safety certification, and follows WHO Good Manufacturing Practices.

Our products are USFDA listed with an FEI code, CDSCO certified and carry European notified CE (4 digit) marking. They are tested to international safety and electromagnetic compatibility standards including IEC 60601-1-2:2014, IEC 61000-6-1:2016, IEC 60335-1:2020 and IEC 60335-2-24:2020, and to IP 69, ASTM (anti corrosion), EN 13523-26, ISO 13849-1 and BIFMA where applicable.
