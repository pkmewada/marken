MarkEn, with 25+ years of expertise, leads in healthcare and medical cold chain solutions, pioneering high-performance refrigeration. As India's only company with 11+ products successfully tested by WHO PQS-accredited laboratories, we hold multiple prestigious accreditations. 01Mission

To Design, Manufacture, And Deliver World-Class, Affordable, And Reliable Healthcare And Cold-Chain Solutions Aligned With The Highest Global Standards. We Are Committed To Protecting Vaccines, Blood, Medicines, And Biologics Through Precision Engineering And Advanced Technologies. By Empowering Governments And Healthcare Institutions, We Enable Safe, Scalable, And Equitable Healthcare Delivery Globally.

02Vision

To Be The World’s No. 1 And Most Trusted Manufacturer Of Medical Cold Chain, Medical Equipment, And Blood Management Solutions. We Aim To Set Global Benchmarks In Quality, Innovation, And Reliability, Ensuring Safe Preservation Of Life Critical Products Worldwide. MarkEn Envisions A Future Where Healthcare Infrastructure Is Resilient, Accessible, And Uncompromised Across Every Geography.
